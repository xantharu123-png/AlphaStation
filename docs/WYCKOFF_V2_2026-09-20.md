# Wyckoff v2: inspectable evidence, not a profitability claim

## Scope

This revision repairs the previously reproduced automatic-reaction pivot and
broken Phase-A-context defects. It adds an explicit event/phase evidence UI and
an offline chronological evaluation harness. It does not enable automatic
trading, relax BI's 17/20 contract, change other scanners, or deploy Hetzner.

The engine is now `causal_wyckoff_v2`. Public result and mail guards reject old
v1 cached Wyckoff rows even if their old score or trade flag was favourable.
The normal next manual scan must recompute evidence; caches are not relabelled.

## Phases and numbering

Canonical Wyckoff phases are A--E. The stopping action in A can include PS/PSY,
SC/BC, AR and a first ST. Repeated secondary tests can occur in B. Optional
Spring/UTAD and subsequent confirmation must not be confused with a mandatory
numbered wave sequence. Our event `sequence` is only a chronological display
ordinal; `occurrence` counts repeated events of the same name. Neither means
Elliott waves or a canonical A1/A2/A3 model.

Reference: [Wyckoff Analytics method](https://www.wyckoffanalytics.com/wyckoff-method/).
The model's numeric thresholds and phase boundaries are implementation
assumptions, not performance-validated constants from that reference.

The first ST is labelled A. Its completed confirmation starts the model's
inferred B interval. Phase intervals include observed and confirmed times;
historical placement never means the event was knowable before confirmation.
Missing C is not invented: a Spring is optional. Phase E, preliminary
support/supply, reaccumulation/redistribution and non-climactic variants are
explicitly outside the current model. They must not silently become signals.
The detector currently records the first qualifying ST per range. The UI can
distinguish repeated ST occurrences when supplied; this is not a claim that a
complete sequence of secondary tests is already detected.

## Recognition changes

- Before the first confirmed ST, stronger completed reaction pivots may replace
  a small intermediate AR pivot. Once the ST confirms, the range is frozen.
- A historical failure of the climax boundary invalidates Phase A even if no
  ST is found and the latest price has recovered.
- A failed confirmed breakout cannot be made valid by choosing a later breakout
  from the same old range. A separate newly established sequence is required.
- Real volume, completed timestamps, opposing-direction exclusion, structural
  plans, invalidation and delivery checks remain mandatory.

## Chart and timeframe contract

Stock scanner signals remain on **1D completed exchange sessions**. The history
loader is asked for 180 observations and can request a reserve of additional
bars; actual valid bars are reported. The minimum is 60 completed bars.
Selected-chart evidence is recomputed on its displayed timeframe; a saved 1D
scanner proof is shown separately and is not passed off as a fresh 1H result.

Chart evidence has dated event prices, separate confirmation dates, inferred
phase intervals and range limits. Context/invalidated patterns are neutral in
colour. Wyckoff evidence is retained outside the generic top-three chart-shape
cap. Range projections are not Point-and-Figure counts, guaranteed objectives,
actual fills, or estimated win probabilities.

## Validation and remaining gates

See [offline validation protocol](WYCKOFF_VALIDATION.md). Synthetic regression
tests demonstrate deterministic behaviour only. Independent labels, unseen
historical periods and prospective results net of real costs are still needed
before claiming reliable market recognition or improved profitability.

No real-data precision, recall, win rate or profit has been inferred from the
unit fixtures. Live deployment and provider/scan completion are separate gates.
